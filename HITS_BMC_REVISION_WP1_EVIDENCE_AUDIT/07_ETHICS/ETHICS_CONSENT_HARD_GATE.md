# Ethics and Consent Gate

**ETHICS_CONSENT_GATE = PASS (author-confirmed; documents not independently inspected).**

The author explicitly confirmed that the study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Xinjiang Medical University (approval no. K202602-10), and that the committee waived the requirement for informed consent because this was a retrospective study. This gate records the author's confirmation; it is not an independent authentication or scope review of the ethics approval documents.

The local manuscript statement “All participants provided informed consent” conflicts with the confirmed waiver and must be corrected in a later manuscript revision. Conceptually locked wording for that future revision is:

> The study was approved by the Ethics Committee of Xinjiang Medical University (approval no. K202602-10). Given the retrospective nature of the study, the requirement for informed consent was waived by the Ethics Committee. All methods were performed in accordance with relevant guidelines and regulations.

Consent for publication: Not applicable.

No ethics approval letter, consent form, patient-level data, or other private ethics attachment is included in this public repository. WP1R updates the ethics gate and flags the inconsistency only; it does not modify the manuscript or authorize WP2.
