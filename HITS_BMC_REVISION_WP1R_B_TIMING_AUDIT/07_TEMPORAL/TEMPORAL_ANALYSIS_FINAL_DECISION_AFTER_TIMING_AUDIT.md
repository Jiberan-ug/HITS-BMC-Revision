# Temporal Analysis: Final Decision After WP1R-B

**Decision: `REMOVE_FROM_MANUSCRIPT` as temporal validation or temporal-transportability evidence.**

The modeled date axis has not been linked to the eligible index coronary-angiography episode. The source lacks an index CAG/PCI date, complete admission/discharge window, and a CBC-to-encounter key. A patient-level visit-token list is derived from inpatient-number fields and does not identify the encounter associated with the selected laboratory row. No machine-verified cohort period or source crosswalk rescues this ordering.

The dates may still encode source data or a stress-test axis, but their clinical meaning is unresolved. Presenting performance across that axis as temporal transportability would overstate the evidence. The analysis and any claim of temporal validation should be omitted from the manuscript when manuscript editing is separately authorized.

No temporal model was rerun, changed, or deleted. All existing frozen numerical artifacts remain intact and unchanged. This decision does not alter the AMI phenotype, frozen model results, or clinical discrimination analyses.
